from our random conversations, silly moments, and late-night talks, hanggang sa simpleng "i love you" natin, every little thing with you means so much to me. even the quiet and ordinary moments feel different when they're shared with you.

loving you is honestly one of the easiest and most natural things i've ever done. i don't need some big reason to love you. i just do. i love you for who you are, for the way you make me feel, for the little things you do without even realizing how much they mean to me, and for simply being the person you are.

i hope simula pa lang to ng something even more beautiful for us c: i can't wait for more months, more memories, more late night conversations, more random moments, more laughter, and all the little things that we'll get to experience together.

whatever the future holds, i'm just really happy that i get to have you in my life and that i get to call you mine :p
